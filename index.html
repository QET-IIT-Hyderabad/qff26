01 / About

From bits to qubits, together.

Qiskit Fall Fest is a worldwide celebration of quantum computing, run by students, for students. |QET⟩ — the Quantum Technologies Club of IIT Hyderabad — brings the fest home, fusing the official IBM Quantum & Qiskit series with our campus community.

Start with the fundamentals, move through expert-led talks, build with Qiskit in the workshops, then turn your ideas into a challenge-ready project. No gatekeeping — beginners, students, researchers and the merely curious are all welcome. Just a shared sky.

02 / Decade on the Cloud

A decade
on the cloud.

Ten years of Qiskit Fall Fest — a decade of making quantum computing open, hands-on and community-run. Here's the flight path that brings it to |QET⟩ IIT Hyderabad.

From local experiments to a world of accessible cloud quantum learning. Qiskit gave the community a common language — now |QET⟩ adds its own accent from IIT Hyderabad.
